FIELD GUIDE

How to play Marble Summit

01 · Get in the game

Roll a 1 or 6 to release a marble from BASE onto your START space. A successful 6 gives an extra roll.

02 · Take the outer loop

Move clockwise using the complete die value. You can pass opponents, but you cannot pass or land on your own marble.

03 · Capture rivals

Land exactly on an opponent to send their marble back to BASE. Capture is optional; choose any legal route.

04 · Use the star shortcuts

If you start a turn on a star, travel clockwise along the star ring or exit toward a different sector to chase opponents. Roll exactly 1 to enter CENTER.

05 · Center portal

From CENTER, roll exactly 1 on a later turn to exit to any star.

06 · Fill HOME

Once a marble has completed its required trip, it may turn into its own HOME lane or keep circulating. HOME marbles can move farther inward on future exact rolls.

A 6 that has no legal move does not earn another roll. First to get all four marbles HOME wins.
