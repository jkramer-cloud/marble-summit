HOW TO PLAY

Marble Summit rules

The race

Each racer has four marbles in their starting base. Roll a 1 or 6 and choose a base marble to put on your start space. On future rolls, move a marble exactly the number of spaces on the die, clockwise around the outside. A 6 earns another roll if that option is enabled.

Blocking and bumping

You may pass an opponent's marble. Landing on one sends that marble to its base. You may never pass or land on your own marbles. Starting bases and home lanes are safe; the start space, shortcut rings, and center are not.

Finishing

Each player has four numbered home recesses. When you reach your own colored HOME ENTRY hole, you can turn into your home lane. The roll must place you in an empty home recess without passing another of your own marbles. First to safely fill all four home spaces wins.

Shortcuts (optional)

Land exactly on a gold star on the outside track. On a later roll you can take its connecting golden route to the inner star holes. Travel clockwise around the inner stars or exit to the matching outer star. The six-sided board adds a second inner route. Shortcuts save distance but expose you to opponents.

The center shortcut

Enter the center from an inner star hole using an exact 1. To leave, roll a 1 (or 1 or 6 if you enable the custom six-player option). Select an available star hole as your exit. Shortcut holes and the center are not safe from captures.

Computer players

Computer racers can be Easy, Medium, or Hard. Each one can be Aggressive, Careful, Shortcut Hunter, Balanced, or Chaotic. Set the animation speed separately from difficulty. When two or more paths end at the same recess, choose the one you want from the move list.

Marble Summit is an independently made marble-racing game inspired by traditional marble races. The extra six-sided shortcut ring, custom player options, and some shortcut timing are original house rules.
